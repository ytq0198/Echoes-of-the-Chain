## Required fidelity surfaces

- Fonts and typography: the reference uses a heavy sans display heading. Iteration 5 intentionally evolves the approved system with self-hosted Noto Serif SC Variable for large Chinese titles while retaining system sans for body copy and monospace for hashes. Font loading, weight, line height, wrapping and mobile scaling were checked.
- Spacing and layout rhythm: homepage proportions remain aligned with the reference. All role pages use a measured 310 px context rail and flexible content column; cards, forms and results share 20 px radii and a 24 px vertical rhythm.
- Colors and visual tokens: paper white, archive beige, deep trust green and muted gold extend the reference without changing semantic status colors. Success and invalid verification states remain distinguishable without relying on color alone.
- Image and asset quality: the reference contains no photographic assets. Icons use Phosphor Vue, the QR code is generated by qrcode.vue, and the display font is self-hosted. No emoji, handcrafted SVG or placeholder graphic replaces a visible asset.
- Copy and content: all labels describe product roles, authorization, ledger evidence or privacy boundaries. No course-development, competition, prompt, demo-user or invented-team copy appears in the UI.

## Comparison history

### Pass 1 — blocked

- [P1] Reviewer detail and student credential were auto-placed into the 310 px context column.
  - Evidence: first real-Fabric reviewer capture in `reports/assets/iteration-5/qa-v1/` and browser geometry showing `.review-sheet` at `left=72`, `width=310`, `height=860`.
  - Impact: hashes wrapped excessively, action buttons became vertical, and the right side contained large empty areas.
  - Fix: introduced an explicit `.workspace-content` wrapper for every role page and forced all panels and results into its single flexible column.

- [P2] Mobile workflow tabs used four 110 px minimum-width items, creating an internal horizontal scrollbar.
  - Evidence: `reports/assets/iteration-5/qa-v1/student-mobile.png`, with tab `clientWidth=359` and `scrollWidth=440`.
  - Fix: removed the mobile minimum width, hid redundant step numbers at this breakpoint and distributed four labels evenly.

### Pass 2 — passed

- Post-fix reviewer geometry: `.review-sheet left=436, width=916`; `.operation-panel left=436, width=916`.
- Post-fix mobile metrics: page `scrollWidth=375`, workflow tabs `clientWidth=375`, `scrollWidth=375` at a 390×844 viewport.
- Home full-view and focused title comparisons show consistent hierarchy, sidebar width, summary density, evidence table and privacy strip.
- The serif display heading is an intentional requested enhancement, not unresolved drift.
- No actionable P0, P1 or P2 findings remain.
