## 观察与瓶颈

- public-verify: highest median logical-operation throughput 746.614/s at concurrency 20; median P95 38.202 ms at that point.
- student-private: highest median logical-operation throughput 752.36/s at concurrency 20; median P95 38.096 ms at that point.
- issue-review: highest median logical-operation throughput 33.883/s at concurrency 20; median P95 700.615 ms at that point.
- batch-1: highest median entry throughput 25.445/s at concurrency 20; median P95 913.708 ms at that point.
- batch-10: highest median entry throughput 167.452/s at concurrency 20; median P95 1325.226 ms at that point.
- batch-25: highest median entry throughput 311.941/s at concurrency 20; median P95 2140.115 ms at that point.
- batch-50: highest median entry throughput 482.549/s at concurrency 20; median P95 2497.371 ms at that point.

84 个正式单元未记录合法请求失败。观察事实是只读吞吐在高并发附近趋于平台，批量提高了条目吞吐，同时较大批次的尾延迟上升。原因推断：并发 5 的部分写负载低于并发 10，可能受到单 orderer 的 2 秒 BatchTimeout、闭环 worker 与成块节奏共同影响；该解释不是额外测量结论。
